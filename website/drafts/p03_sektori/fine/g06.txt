Photorealistic black-and-white press photograph, 1962, large-format camera, silver gelatin print, fine natural grain, luminous grey tones without pure black, real photographic realism, no illustration.
EPIC WIDE SHOT from a hillside far away: a monumental new white concrete arch motorway bridge spanning a deep rocky karst gorge in Croatia dominates the frame, the river far below, mountains behind, calm sky in the upper third.
The opening ceremony is taking place on the bridge deck: a long line of officials, a brass band and a crowd, all small and in realistic proportion to the bridge; across the road at the centre a ceremonial ribbon is being cut.
In the lower right foreground on the rocky hillside, seen from behind at three-quarter view and at realistic scale (about one fifth of the frame height), a surveyor watches the ceremony beside a brass theodolite on a tripod: he is clearly a cyborg, with a polished chrome head plate at the back and side of his head and chrome hands with fine joints, wearing a 1960s work jacket.
Selective color, delicate like a hand-tinted photograph: ONLY the ceremonial ribbon on the bridge is a refined red, small but clearly visible. Everything else black and white. No text, no signs.
