Photorealistic black-and-white press photograph, 1962, large-format camera, silver gelatin print, natural fine grain, true photographic realism, real faces, no illustration.
A packed indoor basketball arena of the early 1960s at night: wooden parquet court, players in period short shorts and canvas sneakers mid-game, a player rising for a jump shot, packed wooden stands, hanging industrial lights, haze in the air. Seen from the courtside officials' table in the foreground: club officials and a lawyer in a slim suit watch the game; beside them the official scorekeeper is a handsome cyborg man with a polished chrome plate at his temple and a chrome hand with fine joints operating the scoring console.
Above the court hangs a period electromechanical scoreboard with a large shot clock.
Selective color, delicate but clearly visible: ONLY the glowing digits of the shot clock and scoreboard are a warm amber. Everything else black and white. No team names, no readable text except digits.
