Outpainting: the photograph in the centre of this canvas is a black-and-white 1962 film still of a confidential meeting in a dark wood-panelled club room. Fill the flat grey border by seamlessly extending the same room outward: more wood panelling, venetian blinds, leather armchairs, the edge of the low table, a bookcase, the floor, in the same light, perspective, grain and photorealism, so the result is one continuous slightly wider shot. Do not change the three people, the cigar's red ember or the bluish smoke. Everything else black and white. No borders, no seams.
