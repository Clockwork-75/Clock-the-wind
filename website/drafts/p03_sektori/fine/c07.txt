Photorealistic black-and-white documentary photograph, 1962, shot by a Magnum-style photojournalist on a Leica, Tri-X film, silver gelatin print, natural fine grain, true photographic realism, natural light, no illustration, no painting.
Late afternoon wheat harvest on a large modern agricultural estate in the flat plains of eastern Croatia. Wide shot. In the middle distance two self-propelled combine harvesters of the early 1960s work in different parts of the field, one unloading grain into a truck driving alongside it; dust in the low sun; a large concrete grain silo complex on the horizon. In the foreground at the edge of the field, at realistic scale: the estate director in shirtsleeves, a grain buyer and a lawyer in a light summer suit stand by a dark sedan, the lawyer holding a leather folder; they watch the harvest, one of them holds a handful of wheat. A small but clearly visible cyborg agronomist with chrome forearms kneels to test the grain with an instrument. Calm sky in the upper third for text.
Selective color, delicate like a hand-tinted photograph: ONLY the ripe wheat is a refined natural golden ochre. Everything else black and white.
