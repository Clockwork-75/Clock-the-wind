The closing of a major M&A transaction at dawn in a high-floor boardroom, the first light of morning in the floor-to-ceiling windows behind them, city skyline. A long walnut table. On each side of the table a principal in an immaculate dark suit sits facing the other and signs the final agreement with a fountain pen; between them stacks of signature pages, closing binders, coffee cups, overflowing ashtrays and loosened paper clips from an all-night session.
Behind each principal stand three tired but proud lawyers of the deal team: jackets off, shirt sleeves rolled up, ties loosened, one rubbing his eyes, one leaning on a chair, a young associate holding the next signature page ready; on one side one of the three is a cyborg associate with a polished chrome plate at his temple and chrome forearms with fine joints visible below the rolled-up sleeves, the other side has no cyborg. Natural, unposed, documentary feeling.
Composition symmetrical across the table, calm upper area of window and sky for a headline.
Selective color, delicate like a hand-tinted photograph: ONLY the fresh fountain-pen ink of the two signatures, a refined deep navy blue, and the warm first sunlight touching the window glass, a faint soft gold. Everything else black and white.
